🌸 Spring Celebrations

"Are you planning a trip to the UK and Ireland next spring? Don't miss out on these celebrations. You are sure to have the time of your life."

🎧 Intro
🎧 ฟังเสียง Hugh
Why not come to Wales for St David's Day? It is on 1st March and last year's celebrations in Cardiff were brilliant. All the girls and boys wore traditional Welsh costumes and sang songs, danced and read poetry. All the buildings in town flew the national flag. We had street parades where people played the Welsh bagpipes. In the evening, I went home for a family dinner. We ate some juicy roast lamb with potatoes. Delicious!
👦 Hugh, 13 (Wales)
🎧 ฟังเสียง James
Don't forget, St Patrick's Day! Try to pop over to Dublin, on 17th of March because the whole city goes crazy. I celebrated with my schoolmates last year and everyone wore shamrocks, green clothes and painted their faces. In the evening people let off fireworks. My mum made some special food. We all ate Irish soda bread, bacon and cabbage. It was yummy. You must come and see for yourself.
👦 James, 12 (Ireland)
🎧 ฟังเสียง Sam
If you are still in the UK on 23rd April, why not visit London for St George's Day. Last year I was in Covent Garden and it was great fun. We watched some medieval street theatre and some people wore dragon costumes. There was even a stall that served roast beef and Yorkshire puddings. We had a great time! Make it a date in your diary!
👦 Sam, 14 (UK)
